# PowerCore — Home Generator & Inverter Service Center

A premium, professional, responsive web platform for **PowerCore**, an industrial-grade Generator & Inverter Service Center.

## 🚀 Key Page Architectures

### 🏛️ Home 1 (`index.html`) — Technical Editorial Foundation
1. **Hero / Power Promise**: Asymmetric technical editorial composition with CAD corner markers, live HUD status monitor, and emergency micro-links.
2. **Trusted Brands / Credibility**: Continuous horizontal brand strip (Cummins, Kirloskar, Mahindra Powerol, Greaves, Honda, Luminous, Microtek, Exide) with metric counters.
3. **Power Solutions by Need**: Expanding interactive horizontal pathways for Home, Shop, and Office setups.
4. **Product Capacity Explorer**: Interactive technical matrix with real-time kVA selector (1 kVA to 15+ kVA) and generator vs. inverter tab switching.
5. **Service Workflow**: 4-Step open-layout connected timeline (*From Breakdown to Back-Up in Four Moves*).
6. **Why Professional Maintenance Matters**: Split editorial section with 6-point diagnostic checklist & interactive maintenance interval selector (Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Annual).
7. **Emergency + AMC Conversion**: High-contrast split-screen featuring immediate 24/7 hotline callout (`1800 123 4567`) and annual maintenance contract overview.
8. **Final Service Booking CTA**: Full-width dark section with animated SVG electrical grid lines and fast-booking trigger.

### ⚡ Home 2 (`home-2.html`) — Immersive Cinematic & Decision Experience
1. **Immersive Power Hero**: Full-width cinematic composition (*"WHEN THE GRID GOES QUIET, YOUR DAY SHOULDN'T"*), atmospheric background, status line, and primary solution pathway.
2. **Power Problem / Solution Story**: Horizontal 5-stage sequential timeline (*Power Cut → Work Stops → Equipment Stops → Time is Lost → Backup Power Takes Over*).
3. **Generator vs. Inverter Decision**: Large interactive decision table comparing Load, Backup Time, Noise, Fuel, Space, and Maintenance with dynamic hover/tap insights.
4. **Product Range Visualizer**: Interactive capacity journey spanning Home (1–5 kVA), Shop (5–15 kVA), Office (10–30 kVA), and Commercial (30+ kVA) with dynamic expansion.
5. **Field Service Story**: 5-stage service explorer (*01 Inspect, 02 Install, 03 Test, 04 Maintain, 05 Respond*) highlighting in-house engineering promises.
6. **Power Reliability Numbers**: Integrated oversized typography statistics with animated counters (`10+ Years`, `1,500+ Systems`, `24/7 Support`, `100% OEM Spares`).
7. **Customer Experience**: Large authentic testimonial quote + 4-step client engagement sequence and trust badges.
8. **Service Booking Conversion**: Natural dark editorial booking section with integrated multi-field dispatch request form.

### 📖 About Page (`about.html`) — Industrial Editorial & Engineering Credibility
1. **Power With Purpose**: Sophisticated off-center editorial introduction, technical status annotations (`ENGINEERED | INSTALLED | MAINTAINED`), and horizontal core spectrum indicator.
2. **Where It Started**: 2012 Year marker + 3 Service-First Principles (*01 Understand, 02 Solve, 03 Support*).
3. **The Power Expertise**: Open technical matrix across Generator Systems, Inverter Systems, Battery Solutions, and Electrical Support with thin dividers.
4. **The Numbers Behind the Work**: Dominant `10+ Years` statistic with hierarchical metrics (`1,500+ Systems`, `24/7 Support`, `Multi-Brand`).
5. **Our Reliability Philosophy**: 6-Stage continuous process sequence (*Listen → Assess → Recommend → Install → Test → Maintain*).
6. **Our Engineering Standard**: Engineering notebook layout with technical scan-line schematic and 6-point checklist.
7. **People Behind the Service**: Interactive team roles explorer (*Service Engineers, Installation Technicians, Diagnostic Specialists, Customer Support, AMC Team*) with dynamic quotes.
8. **What We're Building Next**: Future-facing dark section with 3 commitments (*Smarter Recommendations, Faster Service Response, Longer System Life*) and quick contact actions.

### ⚡ Products Page (`products.html`) — Technical Showroom & Equipment Discovery
1. **Product Introduction**: Technical showroom opening with thin pointer lines and engineered specifications (`OUTPUT`, `FUEL`, `APPLICATION`, `SUPPORT`).
2. **Find Your Power System**: Interactive discovery selector with 4 application rows (*Home, Shop, Office, Commercial*) and dynamic loads/capacity blueprint.
3. **Generator Range**: Vertical capacity lineup (*01 (1–5 kVA) to 05 (100+ kVA)*) with acoustic ratings and live spec inspection.
4. **Inverter Systems**: Animated power-flow particle visualization (*Grid → Inverter → Battery → Load*) with Home/Shop/Office segment switcher.
5. **Battery Matching**: Chemical energy storage comparison (*Tubular Lead-Acid, SMF/VRLA Sealed, Lithium LiFePO4*) across cycle life and discharge rates.
6. **Product Comparison**: Responsive side-by-side engineering comparison table across *Home Basic*, *Business Pro* (Recommended), and *Office Power*.
7. **How to Choose (Decision Tree)**: 4-Question interactive branching calculator (*Space Type, Load Priority, System Preference*) with real-time engineered output.
8. **Equipment Quote & Sizing Engine**: Interactive on-demand equipment console with custom kVA sizing, multi-brand fleet pricing, and instant quotation request form.

### 🔧 Repair Services Page (`services.html`) — Engineering Diagnostics & Workshop Restoration
1. **Repair Introduction**: Asymmetric split opening (*"When Power Fails, We Find Out Why."*) with field diagnostic console schematic (`INPUT`, `OUTPUT`, `LOAD`, `BATTERY`, `ENGINE`, `CONTROL`).
2. **The Symptom Map**: 10-point interactive diagnostic symptom matrix (*Generator Won't Start, Sudden Stops, Low Power, Smoke, Battery Charging, Inverter Dead, Overheat, etc.*) with root cause and priority analysis.
3. **Generator Repair Lab**: 8-zone cutaway inspection sheet (*Engine Block, Fuel System, Cooling, Alternator, Battery, AMF Control, Busbar Wiring, Step-Load Output*).
4. **Inverter & Battery Diagnostics**: 6-stage electrical circuit chain (*Grid Input → Input Filter → Inverter DSP → Battery Bank → Output Stage → Load*) with live diagnostic simulation.
5. **Repair vs Replace**: 3-pathway engineering decision matrix (*01 Repair, 02 Replace Component, 03 Replace System*) with criteria and guidelines.
6. **Our Repair Workflow**: Wide horizontal 6-step engineering ribbon (*Report Issue → Inspect System → Test Components → Identify Cause → OEM Repair → Test Under Load*).
7. **Emergency Repair & Service Priority**: Dark scanning priority matrix (*Critical <45m, High <2h, Standard Scheduled*) and 24/7 hotline dispatch.
8. **Final Repair Booking**: Full-width closing composition with 3 action steps and instant technician dispatch trigger.

### 🛡️ AMC Plans Page (`amc-plans.html`) — Long-Term Power Resilience & Maintenance Program
1. **AMC Hero**: Typographic opening (*"Don’t Wait for Failure."*) with a circular maintenance control dial and service gauge (`INSPECT`, `SERVICE`, `TEST`, `READY`).
2. **Why AMC?**: Cause-and-effect progression comparing unmanaged decay (Without AMC) vs controlled reliability (With AMC).
3. **What Your AMC Covers**: Structured technical maintenance matrix across Generator, Inverter, and Battery systems with standard checkpoints and load tests.
4. **Maintenance Calendar**: 12-Month interactive maintenance schedule (`JAN` to `DEC`) with seasonal service routines for engine, electronics, and battery banks.
5. **Choose the Right AMC**: 3 Full-width horizontal plan bands (*Essential, Professional, Critical Power*) with dependency-based highlighting (*Home, Shop, Office, Critical Operations*).
6. **What Happens During a Visit**: Engineering inspection log document with 8-point verified checklist and verified stamp.
7. **AMC Value / Cost of Downtime**: Oversized business impact statistics (*85% preventable failures, 35% longer lifespan, <45m SLA, 100% OEM parts*) anchored by *"Maintenance is cheaper than interruption."*
8. **AMC Activation CTA**: Immersive circular READY activation closing with 4 assurance states and direct contract enrollment.

### 🗺️ Service Areas Page (`service-areas.html`) — Geographic Coverage & Rapid Dispatch Network
1. **Service Area Hero**: Geographic opening (*"Power Support, Where You Need It."*) with an abstract regional network map, central dispatch hub, and expanding radius rings.
2. **Coverage at a Glance**: Oversized numerical metrics (*24+ Service Hubs, 75 KM Rapid Reach, 1,500+ Active Sites, 24/7 Emergency Dispatch*).
3. **Find Your Service Area**: Interactive location search console with quick filters (*Central Metro, North Industrial, East Commercial, West Highway, South Tech Valley*) and real-time coverage verification.
4. **Service Zone Map**: Interactive regional zone inspector (*Zone 01 Central, Zone 02 North, Zone 03 East, Zone 04 West*) with live SLA and mobile van availability.
5. **Service by Location Type**: 4 Location-context horizontal bands (*Residential Homes, Retail Shops, Corporate Offices, Industrial Commercial Complexes*) with dynamic recommendation bar.
6. **How Service Reaches You**: 5-Stop winding route journey (*Your Location → Dispatch Triage → Transit Route → On-Site Audit → Verified Complete*).
7. **Outside the Standard Zone**: Extended reach reassurance protocol for remote sites and custom industrial fleet routing.
8. **Final Service Area CTA**: Converging route geographic closing with direct booking and 24/7 emergency dispatch hotline.

### 📞 Contact & Booking Page (`contact.html`) — Service Intake Command Center
1. **Contact Hero**: Service intake opening (*"Tell Us What Your Power System Needs."*) with a visual command status console (`READY TO HELP` across Generator, Inverter, Battery, AMC, Installation).
2. **Choose Your Request**: Interactive 8-option intake selector (*Repair, Maintenance, AMC, Installation, Battery, Inverter, Generator, General Enquiry*) with dynamic recommendation sheets.
3. **Service Booking Form**: 4-Step multi-step booking engine (*01 Details → 02 Equipment → 03 Service Need → 04 Review & Confirm*) with live validation and summary generator.
4. **Emergency Support**: Visually dominant dark section featuring direct 24/7 toll-free dispatch (`1800 123 4567`).
5. **Other Ways to Reach Us**: Multi-channel communication rows (*Direct Phone, WhatsApp Chat, Email Desk, Workshop Visit*).
6. **Before You Book**: 4-Category preparation checklist (*Equipment, Problem, Location, Access*) to streamline on-site arrival.
7. **After You Submit**: 5-Stage request progression status bar (*Received → Reviewed → Confirmed → In Transit → Verified*) with an interactive request ID demo lookup.
8. **Final Contact / Booking CTA**: Signal-to-service closing with direct online dispatch and phone hotline links.
