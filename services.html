05 // TECHNICAL DECISION

Repair It. Replace It. Or Maintain It?

We provide transparent engineering assessments so you never spend money on unviable machines.

01

REPAIR

RECOMMENDED WHEN:
  • Component failure is isolated (AVR, starter, fuse)
  • Engine compression and alternator coils are healthy
  • Total repair cost is under 30% of replacement value
02

REPLACE COMPONENT

RECOMMENDED WHEN:
  • Battery bank has exceeded 4-5 year cycle lifetime
  • Alternator diode bridge or radiator is beyond repair
  • Upgrading to LiFePO4 for 3x longer operating life
03

REPLACE SYSTEM

RECOMMENDED WHEN:
  • Equipment is outdated (>12 years) with obsolete spares
  • Building load has grown beyond current kVA ceiling
  • Repeated major engine breakdowns cause business loss
“We diagnose first. We recommend second.”
